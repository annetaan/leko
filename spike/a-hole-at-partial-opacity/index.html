Does a mask hole at partial opacity dim the hole by that much?

Each hole is a data: SVG image whose rectangle is drawn at a fill-opacity below 1, subtracted from a scrim of rgb(0 0 0 / 0.45). Beside each is a swatch painted at the alpha the hole should leave. Where the two match, the hole dims by exactly what its opacity says.

Case 3: a dip

One hole whose opacity is rewritten every frame, a new data: URL each time, along (1 + cos 2πp) / 2 over 400ms: open, closed halfway, open again. Judged by eye: it should read as one smooth dip, with no flicker and no frame where the hole blinks to bare or to fully dimmed out of turn.

Read from a screenshot

Judge by eye when the page is opened by hand, Safari included: a page cannot read its own pixels, so the table below stays empty. Each hole should look exactly like the swatch beside it. run.mjs hands the page a screenshot of itself, and the page reads that and fills the table: it averages a block of device pixels at each sample point and at the matching point of its reference, and passes the sample when no channel is more than levels in 255 off.
