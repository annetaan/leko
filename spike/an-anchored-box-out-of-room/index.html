Where does an anchored box go once the side it is on has no room?

A box anchored to a zero-area marker on the edge of a hole is meant to stay on that side of the edge, whatever the scroll does. The question is what the browser does once that side has no room for it — at the draw, after a scroll, after the box's words change, and after the marker is moved by a style write, the way a loop that follows a pinned target moves it — and whether any spelling keeps the box off the hole in all of them.

Four spellings, each drawn and put through the same six events:

  • area — position-area: bottom center (or top center) with the gap as a margin toward the hole.
  • area + fallbacks — the same, plus position-try-fallbacks: flip-block, flip-inline.
  • inset — top: anchor(bottom) (or bottom: anchor(top)) with justify-self: anchor-center, auto on the other three insets, the same margin, and no fallbacks.
  • inset, no autos — the same without the autos, so the popover's UA inset: 0 holds the other three edges.

Every cell is the box's vertical extent on screen and a verdict: on the hole where the box and the hole overlap at all, and clear where they do not. A box that is clear may still be off screen; that is the price the question is about, and the extent says where it went.

The events

The first three are the same draw: the hole runs from 40px below the top of the viewport to 40px above its foot, the box is drawn below it, and no side has room. They differ only in the document around it.

  1. no room, cannot scroll — the document is exactly one viewport tall.
  2. no room, at scroll 0 — the document is three viewports tall, made so by a block in its flow, and has not been scrolled. Events 3 to 6 are in this document too.
  3. no room, scrolled first — the same document, scrolled by a viewport's height before the draw, with the hole at the same place on screen.
  4. scrolled out of room — at scroll 0, a 200px hole drawn 400px down with the box above it, where there is room, and the document then scrolled until the hole's top edge is 40px from the top of the viewport.
  5. words changed — after that, the box's text is replaced with a longer one, so it is laid out again.
  6. marker moved — event 4 drawn afresh, and then, instead of the words changing, the hole and the marker both moved 20px up by writing their top, with no scroll.
