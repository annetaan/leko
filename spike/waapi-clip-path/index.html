clip-path animation — WAAPI vs requestAnimationFrame

Both panels animate the same clip-path geometry over the same duration. The left one is driven by element.animate(); the right one writes the same interpolation frame by frame from requestAnimationFrame. No libraries. Press Run and compare the two while they are animating — not after they stop. On a 2x display the left panel's dark area covers only part of its stage.
