Do an outline and an outer shadow stay out of the box they decorate?

The halo frames a cutout by sitting a transparent element exactly on the hole and decorating it with outline and an outer box-shadow. That is only allowed if neither puts paint inside the box: the hole shows the real page, and the halo must not paint over it. CSS says both stay outside — an outline is drawn outside the border edge (css-ui-3), and an outer shadow is clipped out of the border box (css-backgrounds-3) — and this page watches a browser do it.

Negative offset and inset shadow, for contrast

What straying paint looks like: the ring and the glow sit on the checkerboard. A host that writes a negative --leko-halo-offset or an inset shadow gets this, and is choosing it.

Judging the hit…

The paint half has to be judged by eye — nothing dependency-free can read the pixels back. The hit half judges itself: with pointer-events: none on the frame, a hit inside it must land on the target underneath.
