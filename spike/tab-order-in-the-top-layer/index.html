Where does a popover sit in the tab order?

Five buttons in the page, then a popover holding two more. The popover is last in the document and paints in the top layer. Press Tab from the top and the readout says the order focus actually took.

Verdict

Click 1 page and hold Tab down. A popover that keeps its DOM position gives the two lines the same order, which means a box painted over everything is still reached last.
