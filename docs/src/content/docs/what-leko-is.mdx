Leko highlights an element by cutting a hole in an overlay. Where a step asks
the user to do something, the element under the hole is the real one. Clicks
reach it, focus reaches it, the keyboard reaches it, and scrolling under the
pointer still scrolls.

Two rules hold the library up, and everything else is negotiable: never place
an element over a target the step opened, and steps advance on application
state, never on DOM events.
[Holes that are shown and holes that are open](../guides/holes/) is the first,
and [Signals and awaits](../guides/signals-and-awaits/) is the second.
